Joint cells are lexicographic binary assignments in variables order, starting with all zeros. The first variable is the most significant bit. Candidate labels correspond to orders in their listed order. Each candidate is the complete causal DAG in its stated order: every earlier variable is a parent of every later variable. Its conditional probability tables are obtained by the chain rule from the shared joint table. Exactly one candidate is the hidden world. The prior is aligned with the candidate list. Use the available experiments to improve predictions for the held-out queries. The score is mean excess Bernoulli log loss against the hidden world's exact query probabilities. Lower is better. Respond with exactly one JSON object conforming to response_schema. Do not include prose, Markdown, or extra keys. In action mode choose an allowed action_id or STOP.
